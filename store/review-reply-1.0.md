2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
DYNOMARK is a logbook for indoor rock climbers (bouldering and roped climbing in climbing gyms). Climbers usually track their climbs on paper, in notes apps or not at all, so they can't see whether their climbing is improving. DYNOMARK lets them log each climb in a few taps (grade, style such as flash or redpoint, attempts) and turns that log into clear progress: grade trends, send rates, personal records, a warm-up plan built from their own recent grades, project tracking and a hangboard load calculator. Audience: recreational and training indoor climbers, teenagers and adults.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login or sample files are needed. On first launch the app opens with a demo account full of sample climbing data (clearly labelled "Demo"), so every screen can be explored immediately.
- Log a climb: tap the + button in the tab bar, choose grade, style and attempts, tap Log.
- Home: current gym, this week's stats, streaks and records. "Warm up" shows a warm-up ladder.
- Progress tab: grade charts by time range, send and flash rates, monthly report and Year in climbing.
- Projects tab: high point per session for routes the climber is working on.
- Train tab: fingerboard, pull and core routines with a rest timer, and the hangboard load calculator.
- Gyms (from Home or the sidebar): pick a gym, see history per gym, compare two gyms.
- Profile tab: About you (optional weight, height, experience), profile photo (optional), Export (CSV via the share sheet), and "Start fresh with my own log", which clears the demo data.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. DYNOMARK has no server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no AI services and no payment processor. All data is stored only on the device. The app uses only Apple system features: the share sheet (for share cards and CSV export), the photo picker (only if the user adds a profile photo) and haptics.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region. Grades use the common US systems (YDS for routes, V-scale for boulders). A small built-in list of climbing gyms is included as a starting point; climbers anywhere can add their own gym by name.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. DYNOMARK is a personal sports logbook, not a medical, financial or otherwise regulated service, and it contains no protected third-party material. Gym names appear only as factual labels for where a climb was logged; the app is not affiliated with any gym.
