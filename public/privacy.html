Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026 (first version 23 November 2026) · applies to the DYNOMARK app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to the web version at studio-vektor.com/send/

In short: your climbing log stays on your device. If you join Crew (optional, from version 1.3), we store your username and the session summaries you choose to post, so your accepted friends can see them. If you buy DYNOMARK Pro, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment and our purchase service keeps a record of what you bought, under a random ID, so Pro stays unlocked. We don't sell or share your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

DYNOMARK is made by Studio Vektor (studio-vektor.com). For anything about privacy, write to mo.elkhalloufi@studio-vektor.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following on your device only, in the app's own storage:

  • Your climbing log: climbs, sessions, projects, gyms you pick, notes and training workouts.
  • Profile details you choose to add: display name, photo, body weight, height, gender and experience level. All of these are optional.
  • Settings such as theme and units.

This data never leaves your device unless you send it yourself (see "Sharing and export"). We cannot see it, back it up or restore it.

Photos and camera

The app asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to add a photo. A small, resized copy of the photo you pick is saved on your device. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

DYNOMARK Pro purchases

DYNOMARK Pro (from app version 1.1) is sold through Apple's App Store and Google Play. They take the payment; we never see your card details, name, email address or Apple ID / Google account.

To know whether Pro is unlocked, and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), a service that checks purchases with the store. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user ID created on your device (not your name, email or a device advertising ID),
  • your purchase history for DYNOMARK: which plan, price, currency, store country, dates, renewals, trials, cancellations and refunds, as reported by the store,
  • basic technical details needed to talk to the store, such as app version, operating system and IP address.

We use this only to unlock Pro and to see how many people buy which plan. It is never combined with your climbing log, which stays on your device, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. The app checks your Pro status when it starts, so RevenueCat sees the random ID even if you never buy; in that case there is no purchase history to send. The web version doesn't contact RevenueCat.

Founding members (people who first downloaded DYNOMARK by 2 December 2026) keep Pro without buying it. To recognise them on a new phone or after a reinstall, the app asks the App Store, through RevenueCat, which version of DYNOMARK your Apple ID first downloaded and when; the answer is also saved on your device.

To cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. To ask us to delete your purchase record, write to the address above.

Crew (optional)

Crew lets friends see each other's sessions. It's off until you join, and the rest of the app works the same without it. When you join, you sign in with Apple. Apple tells us a random account ID, and your email address only if you choose to share it (Apple can hide it); we don't use the email for anything.

The Crew server (hosted by Supabase, supabase.com, on our behalf under its privacy policy) stores:

  • your username, and the first name and home gym if you add them;
  • your friend requests, friends and blocked climbers;
  • each session summary you post: the date, how many climbs and sends, and only the details you switched on (gym, duration, grades and grade pyramid, attempts and first-try sends, a project send);
  • the reactions you give (Allez, Send it, Crusher, Clean, Respect) and any report you send about another climber.

Only friends you accepted can see your posts. Other signed-in climbers can find your username and home gym in search, unless you blocked each other. There are no comments, messages or public profiles. Your notes, body weight, height, photos and the rest of your log are never sent. We read reports to keep Crew safe and may remove accounts that break the rules.

You can remove a post at any time, and delete your Crew account in Crew › Sharing & privacy › Delete Crew account. That deletes your username, posts, reactions, friends and reports you sent from the server right away; your log on the phone stays.

Sharing and export

When you tap Share or Export, the app creates an image or a CSV file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next (Messages, email, Files, a social app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

  • No account needed. Crew sign-in is optional.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no data brokers. (RevenueCat, above, only handles purchases.)
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking. Gyms are chosen by you from a list.

The web version

The web version is served from studio-vektor.com. Like any website, our hosting provider processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages. We don't add analytics to DYNOMARK, and its fonts are served from our own site, not third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or, for the web version, clear the site's data in your browser) and everything it stored is removed. You can also clear your log in the app under Profile → "Start fresh".

Children

DYNOMARK is not directed at children under 13, and Crew is only for people 13 and older. Without Crew we don't collect climbing data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. This version describes Crew, which arrives with app version 1.3.
