Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · applies to the DYNOMARK app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to the web version at studio-vektor.com/send/

In short: DYNOMARK has no accounts and no servers that receive your climbing data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't collect, sell or share your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

DYNOMARK is made by Studio Vektor (studio-vektor.com). For anything about privacy, write to mo.elkhalloufi@studio-vektor.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following on your device only, in the app's own storage:

  • Your climbing log: climbs, sessions, projects, gyms you pick, notes and training workouts.
  • Profile details you choose to add: display name, photo, body weight, height, gender and experience level. All of these are optional.
  • Settings such as theme and units.

This data never leaves your device unless you send it yourself (see "Sharing and export"). We cannot see it, back it up or restore it.

Photos and camera

The app asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to add a photo. A small, resized copy of the photo you pick is saved on your device. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing and export

When you tap Share or Export, the app creates an image or a CSV file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next (Messages, email, Files, a social app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no data brokers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking. Gyms are chosen by you from a list.

The web version

The web version is served from studio-vektor.com. Like any website, our hosting provider processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages. We don't add analytics to DYNOMARK, and its fonts are served from our own site, not third parties.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or, for the web version, clear the site's data in your browser) and everything it stored is removed. You can also clear your log in the app under Profile → "Start fresh".

Children

DYNOMARK is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If DYNOMARK ever adds accounts or online sync, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
