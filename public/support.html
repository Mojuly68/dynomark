Support

DYNOMARK: log routes and boulders fast, track grades, projects and training across gyms.

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mo.elkhalloufi@studio-vektor.com. Please include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. DYNOMARK has no accounts or cloud sync, so your climbing data is never stored on our side. (If you buy Pro, only the purchase record is kept, to unlock Pro.) See the privacy policy.

How do I keep my log when I change phones?

Open Profile → "Export climbing history (CSV)" and save the file somewhere safe (Files, email, cloud drive). The app can't import it back yet, so keep the export as your backup.

The app opened with sample data

New installs start with a demo account so the charts have something to show. It's labelled "Demo" everywhere. Go to Profile → "Start fresh with my own log" to clear it.

What counts as a warm-up?

Anything you switch "Warm-up" on for. Warm-ups still count as climbs and sends, but they're left out of your grade averages, flash rate and attempts per send.

Why do grades differ between gyms?

Gym grades aren't standardized. On a gym's page you can mark its grades as soft or hard, and gym comparisons can take that into account. Everything is based only on your own logs.

DYNOMARK Pro: restore, cancel, refunds

Logging, warm-ups, projects, training and CSV export are free. Pro (app version 1.1 and later) adds long-range progress, monthly and yearly reports, gym comparisons, hangboard history and every share-card style.

  • New phone or reinstalled? Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play, not by us.
  • Founding members: if you first downloaded DYNOMARK by Tuesday 2 December 2026 (Pacific time), Pro is free for you, for life, from version 1.2. It's tied to your Apple ID, so it comes back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling: open the app (or tap "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen) while online.

How do I delete everything?

Profile → "Start fresh" clears the log. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
